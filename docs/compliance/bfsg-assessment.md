# BFSG scope assessment

**Question:** Does the Barrierefreiheitsstärkungsgesetz (BFSG) apply to this site?

**Assessment:** Very likely not.

**Date:** 2026-09-03 · **Reassess if:** the site starts selling anything, takes bookings
for a paid service, or becomes part of a commercial offering.

---

## Reasoning

The BFSG has applied since 28 June 2025. It obliges *economic operators* to make certain
products and services accessible, and the relevant service category here would be
electronic commerce — services that let a consumer conclude a contract online.

This site does none of that:

- Nothing is sold, and no contract can be concluded on it.
- There is no consumer-facing service; it is a personal CV published to be read.
- The contact form and the Calendly booking are ways to start a conversation with an
  individual, not a transaction for a service offered to consumers.

The micro-enterprise exemption for services would in any case apply to a single
individual with no employees and no turnover from the site.

## Why this is written down

Recording the reasoning is cheaper than the two alternatives: building to a standard
that isn't owed, or being unable to explain the decision later if anyone asks. The
assessment is also the kind of thing that is easy to get wrong by drift — hence the
reassessment trigger above.

## What is done anyway

Accessibility work happens here because it is good practice, not because the BFSG
compels it. The site already carries:

- Semantic landmarks and a skip-to-content link.
- Labelled interactive controls, including icon-only buttons.
- Visible focus states and keyboard-operable dialogs.
- `prefers-reduced-motion` honoured for the hero parallax.
- Both light and dark themes with deliberate contrast.

None of this is a compliance claim. It is not a WCAG conformance statement, and no
audit against WCAG 2.1 AA has been carried out.

## Not legal advice

This is an engineering assessment recorded for traceability. If the site's purpose
changes, get the question reviewed rather than relying on this note.
